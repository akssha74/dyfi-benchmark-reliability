Figure 1. Flow diagram for qualifying a candidate model. A candidate passes
through schema and leakage checks, frozen role and temporal-holdout checks, and
proper-score comparison with a paired interval. Failed audits make a candidate
inadmissible; admitted candidates end as either a supported improvement or an
unresolved comparison.

Figure 2. Cohort flow from 7,520 frozen USGS DYFI records to 2,362 eligible
events. Eligible events are assigned to 1,573 training, 357 development, and
430 temporal-holdout roles, with two temporal-straddle exclusions.

Figure 3. Two-panel sensitivity figure. The left panel shows prevalence, Brier
score, and AUROC at severe-intensity thresholds 5.5, 6.0, and 6.5. The right
panel compares Brier score and AUROC for the 2023 and 2024 portions of the
temporal holdout.

Figure 4. Two-panel zero-based bar comparison of random-event, event-grouped,
and sequence-grouped cross-validation for the fixed random-forest diagnostic.
Sequence grouping has slightly lower Brier score and slightly higher AUROC than
random-event assignment; the contrast is descriptive and has no retained
interval.

Figure 5. Forest plot of temporal-holdout Brier scores and 95% sequence-cluster
bootstrap intervals for B0, B1, B2, B4, and B5. Every learned baseline is below
the no-skill B0 score, while the leading learned-model intervals overlap.
