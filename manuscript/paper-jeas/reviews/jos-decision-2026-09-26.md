## Editor decision

Following editorial assessment and an external expert opinion, the journal
declined the manuscript. The editor found that, although the paper presented an
elaborate reproducible and leakage-audited DYFI benchmark, its scientific
objective, seismological significance, and usefulness were insufficiently
clear. The contribution appeared to be primarily a machine-learning evaluation
protocol rather than a substantive advancement in seismology.

## External expert comment

> I have read the article but I honestly could not understand which is the goal
> of the study and its usefulness.

## Binding remediation

The JEAS retarget must not treat this as a formatting complaint. It must:

1. state the engineering model-comparison question in one sentence;
2. name intended users and the decision the benchmark enables;
3. demonstrate use with the frozen baseline results;
4. identify the invalid engineering claims prevented by each gate;
5. preserve all non-use boundaries and null/reversed findings; and
6. pass an independent 30-second editor test before submission.
