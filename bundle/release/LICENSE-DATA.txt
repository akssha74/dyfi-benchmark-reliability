Source data provenance
----------------------
The underlying data are USGS "Did You Feel It?" (DYFI) and the ComCat event
catalogue (U.S. Geological Survey), which are US-Government works in the public
domain (CC0-compatible), cite DOI 10.5066/F7J101C8. No respondent-level personally
identifiable information is redistributed: only event-level source metadata,
derived labels, non-predictive eligibility fields, role assignments, and
per-event provenance hashes are released. Reconstruct the exact hash-pinned
snapshot via the fetch-and-verify source-locator manifest.
